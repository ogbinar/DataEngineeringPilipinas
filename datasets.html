Open Data Sources

“A collection of Philippine & International data sources that can be freely used for projects.”

Philippine Data Sources

Government, Public Administration, and Census Data

  • Philippine Standard Geographic Code (PSGC) - Comprehensive list of administrative divisions in the Philippines.
  • Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) OpenStat - Various statistical datasets provided by the Philippine Statistics Authority.
  • PSA Census of Population and Housing (CPH) - Official population counts.
  • PSA Annual Poverty Indicators Survey (APIS) - Annual data on poverty indicators in the Philippines.
  • PSA Family Income and Expenditure Survey (FIES) - Information on family incomes and expenditures in the Philippines.
  • PSA Poverty Statistics - Official poverty statistics and indices.
  • Cities and Municipalities Competitiveness Index (CMCI) - Data assessing the competitiveness of Philippine cities and municipalities.
  • Unified Accounts Code Structure (UACS) - Standard for government accounting in the Philippines.
  • Open Data Philippines (beta) - Official portal for various government datasets in the Philippines.
  • Philippines - Subnational Administrative Boundaries - Data on administrative boundaries within the Philippines.
  • Awesome Data Philippines by BNHR - Curated collection of Philippine data resources.
  • Philippines Population Projection 2020-2025 (Admin3) - Population projections at subnational levels.

Social Welfare (DSWD) & Inclusion

  • Report on 4Ps DSWD Briefer - Overview of the Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program.
  • DSWD Major Programs and Projects Beneficiaries (2020) - Data on beneficiaries of DSWD programs.
  • 4Ps Fourth Quarter Accomplishment Report - Recent performance and accomplishments of the 4Ps program.
  • DSWD PDPB Statistics - Policy Development and Planning Bureau statistical reports.

Economic, Finance, and Stock Market Data

  • Economic and Social Database (PIDS) - Economic and social data provided by the Philippine Institute for Development Studies.
  • World Bank Philippines Data - Economic data and indicators for the Philippines from the World Bank.
  • BSP Banks Directory - Directory of banks and financial institutions operating in the Philippines.
  • BSP Financial Service Access Points - Listing of financial service access points.
  • Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) General Site - Central bank data and financial policies.
  • Trading Economics - Philippines Stock Market - Real-time and historical stock market data.
  • Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE) Edge - Official disclosure portal of the PSE.
  • Simply Wall St - Philippines - Philippine stock market analysis and insights.
  • TradingView - PSEI - Charting and financial data for the PSE Composite Index.
  • Investing.com - PSE Composite Historical Data - Historical financial metrics for the PSEi.
  • Inquirer Business - Stock Market News Impact - Example news data affecting market capitalization.

Infrastructure and Civil Works

  • DPWH Infrastructure Projects - Database of Department of Public Works and Highways projects.
  • BetterGov Flood Control Projects - Tracking of flood control initiatives.
  • Google Trends - Flood Control Project (PH) - Public search interest on flood control.

Public Health Data

  • COVID Data via DOH Data Drop - Official Department of Health repository for COVID-19 data.
  • DOH Health Facilities Database - Official registry of health facilities in the Philippines.
  • List of Hospitals in the Philippines (Wikipedia) - Open-source compilation of PH hospitals.
  • Dengue Cases in the Philippines (Kaggle) - Granular datasets regarding historical dengue outbreaks.
  • WHO Immunization Data - Philippines - Vaccination coverage and statistics.

Education Data

  • Department of Education Open Data - Data on school characteristics and assessment outcomes at school- and division-level.
  • List of Colleges and Universities in the Philippines - Directory of higher education institutions.
  • List of State Schools, Colleges and Universities - Focus on publicly-funded higher education.
  • Category: Lists of Universities and Colleges (PH) - Assorted localized lists of academic institutions.

OFW and Migration Data

  • CFO: Statistical Profile of Registered Filipino Emigrants (1981-2020) - Long-term emigration data from the Commission on Filipinos Overseas.
  • CFO: Statistical Profile of Spouses & Partners of Foreign Nationals (1989-2020) - Data on CFO-registered marriage migrants.

International & Global Data Sources

General, Multipurpose, and Governance

  • World Bank Development Indicators - Primary collection of development indicators compiled from officially-recognized international sources.
  • Worldwide Governance Indicators - Data on governance quality for over 200 countries.
  • UN Statistics Division - Global statistical databases and publications.
  • CIA World Factbook - Essential global facts and demographics.
  • GADM Data - Detailed maps and data of administrative areas of countries (GIS).
  • Google Datasets Cloud & Google Dataset Search - Search engines and hosting for vast dataset arrays.
  • BigQuery Public Datasets - Large public datasets queryable via SQL.

Environment, Climate, and Geography

  • Our World in Data (Philippines filtered) - Extensive global research and data, filterable by country.
  • Open-Meteo - Open-source weather API and historical weather data.
  • Berkeley Earth Policy Insights - Data on global temperature and climate change.
  • Global Forest Watch - Real-time data and tracking of global deforestation.
  • EM-DAT International Disaster Database - Core database on global natural and technological disasters.
  • NOAA Climate & Weather Data - Extensive datasets from the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.
  • HydroSheds Data - Hydrological data and high-resolution mapping.
  • Earthquake Data (USGS) - Seismic activity tracking.
  • Typhoon Data (Typhoon2000) - Archival and tracking data for Western Pacific typhoons.

Economy, Infrastructure & Competitiveness

  • OECD Data Explorer - Indicators on economics, education, and environment from the OECD.
  • World Bank GDP per Capita (PPP) - Economic comparative data.
  • WEF Global Competitiveness Report - Assessment of the drivers of productivity and long-term economic growth.
  • IEA Data and Statistics - Global energy market data.
  • WASH Data (Household) - Global metrics on water, sanitation, and hygiene.
  • Numbeo Cost of Living - Global database of consumer prices and standard of living metrics.

Public Health & Life Sciences

  • WHO Global Health Collections - Global health statistics.
  • WHO Triple Billion Dashboard - Progress towards global health goals.
  • Global Burden of Disease Data (IHME) - Comprehensive metrics on global mortality and disability.
  • Our World in Data: Annual Deaths by Cause - Historical mortality data.
  • Our World in Data: Global Vaccination Coverage - Immunization trends.
  • Healthcare Images (Google Cloud) - Medical imaging public datasets.
  • Genome Datasets - Data from the International Genome project.

Education and Inequality

  • UNESCO UIS Data Browser - Global education, science, and culture statistics.
  • World Inequality Database on Education (WIDE) - Highlights disparities in education across countries.
  • Human Capital Index (Wikipedia) - Summaries and sources for human capital rankings.

Graft, Corruption, and Human Rights

  • Transparency International CPI - Corruption Perceptions Index ranking global public sector corruption.
  • UNU-WIDER World Income Inequality Database - Comprehensive data on income inequality globally.
  • V-Dem Human Rights Index (OWID) - Varieties of Democracy project scores.
  • UCDP (Uppsala Conflict Data Program) - Data on organized violence and armed conflict.
  • ACLED Data - Armed Conflict Location & Event Data Project.
  • UNODC Data - United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime statistics.

Travel, Tourism, and Migration

  • UN Tourism Statistics - Global inbound, outbound, and domestic tourism data.
  • World Bank Tourism Data - International tourism metrics.
  • OECD Tourism Receipts and Spending - Economic impact of tourism.
  • World Tourism Rankings (Wikipedia) - Open aggregation of top tourist destinations.
  • Henley Passport Index - Global passport strength and visa-free access rankings.
  • BetterGov Travel Visa Data - Requirements and visa statistics.
  • World Migration Report (IOM) - Trends and numbers of international migrants.
  • UN International Migrant Stock - Data on foreign-born populations globally.

Entertainment, Movies, and Music

  • IMDb Non-Commercial Datasets - Bulk datasets containing movie, TV, and crew information.
  • Box Office Mojo - Box office tracking and historical gross data.
  • The Numbers (Movie Budgets & Box Office) - Detailed financial data for the film industry.
  • Rotten Tomatoes Movies Dataset (Reddit) - Community-shared dataset of movie ratings.
  • OMDb API - Open Movie Database API for fetching film details.
  • Wikidata SPARQL Queries - Queryable graph database containing vast entertainment metadata.
  • GWI Music Streaming Around the World - Insights and reports on music consumption habits.
  • Almost Million Songs Dataset (Kaggle) - Large-scale dataset with audio features.
  • Spotify Music Dataset (DataCamp) - Data for analyzing Spotify trends.
  • Spotify Charts Dataset (Kaggle) - Historical ranking of top songs globally and regionally.

Machine Learning, Tech & Open Source

  • Scikit-Learn Toy Datasets - Standard datasets included in Scikit-Learn for ML practice.
  • PyTorch Datasets - Datasets readily available in the PyTorch vision library.
  • Huggingface Datasets - Massive hub for NLP, Vision, and Audio datasets.
  • Wikipedia ML Research Datasets - Directory of common ML benchmarking datasets.
  • Academic Torrents - BitTorrent-based platform for sharing extremely large research datasets.
  • Kaggle Datasets Hub - Thousands of user-uploaded datasets for data science.
  • data.world - Collaborative data catalog and sharing platform.
  • Datahub - Resource and hosting instance for data engineering.
  • Public API Lists for Data Engineering - Directory of public APIs for building data pipelines.
  • ITU Statistics / ITU-D - Global data on telecommunication and ICT developments.
